# SOF NGO Presence Update

This Vercel-ready package updates the existing Sam Oyus Foundation partnership portal with:
- Clear Nigerian NGO/non-profit identity and grassroots positioning.
- Nationwide service vision (36 states + FCT) and future Africa expansion ambition, carefully stated as goals rather than unsupported claims of existing operations.
- About SOF, mission, vision and empowerment approach.
- Past-activity gallery using the supplied medical outreach and vocational-training images.
- Documented education impact: 5,000 writing materials distributed to primary-school pupils in Modakeke (reported October 2023).
- Documented vocational support to House of Akoraye graduates, including working tools/equipment.
- Publicly announced ₦100m programme commitment, explicitly described as an announced commitment rather than full disbursement.
- Transparency/partnership section.
- Existing interventions, proposals, sponsor registration, dashboard and grant-opportunity functions retained.

Public-source research used for editorial verification included Vanguard and Tribune Online reporting from 2023. Healthcare photos/activity descriptions are based on images supplied directly by SOF; no unsupported patient counts are stated.
