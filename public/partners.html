WAYS TO PARTNER

Different resources. One shared mission.

SOF seeks partnerships that combine funding, expertise, technology, equipment and institutional collaboration to create measurable, sustainable community impact.

01

Grant Funding

Fund defined programmes with clear objectives, beneficiary groups, budgets, implementation plans and reporting requirements.

02

Corporate & CSR Sponsorship

Sponsor education, youth skills, agriculture, enterprise, health and community-development interventions aligned with corporate social-impact priorities.

03

International Donor Partnership

Work with SOF on community programmes designed around local needs and responsible implementation in Nigeria.

04

In-Kind Support

Provide learning materials, ICT equipment, vocational tools, agricultural inputs, medical resources, facilities or other programme resources.

05

Technical Partnership

Contribute expertise in monitoring and evaluation, technology, health, agriculture, education, research, entrepreneurship, programme design or institutional development.

06

Strategic Collaboration

Partner on research, programme development, capacity building, community engagement and scalable social-impact initiatives.
